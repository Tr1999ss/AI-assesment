# Expense Policy

## Submitting claims
Submit expense claims in the expense system within thirty days of the purchase date. Claims submitted later
than ninety days are rejected unless the finance team grants an exception in writing.

## Receipts
Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A photo of the receipt is accepted; credit card statements alone are
not. If a receipt is lost, complete the missing-receipt declaration, which your manager must countersign.

## Approval limits
Claims up to 500 EUR are approved by your line manager. Claims between 500 and 5,000 EUR also need the
department head. Anything above 5,000 EUR requires pre-approval from the finance director before spending.

## Corporate cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel or purchase regularly. Personal spending on a corporate
card is not allowed; any accidental personal charge must be repaid within fourteen days.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll run. Claims approved after the 20th of the month
are paid in the following month's payroll.
