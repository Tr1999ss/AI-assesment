# Pricing FAQ

## Plans
PLN-STARTER-01 is the Starter plan for up to five users. PLN-TEAM-01 is the Team plan for up to fifty users
with single sign-on. PLN-ENTERPRISE-01 is the Enterprise plan with unlimited users and a dedicated success
manager.

## Discounts
Annual billing is discounted by fifteen percent compared with monthly billing. Registered non-profits and
schools receive an additional twenty percent discount on any plan.

## Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current month. Annual plans cancelled
within thirty days of purchase are refunded in full; after that, no refund is given.

## Billing cycle
Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and are payable within fourteen days by card or
bank transfer.
