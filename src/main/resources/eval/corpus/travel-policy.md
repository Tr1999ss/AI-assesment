# Travel Policy

## Booking flights
Book all flights through the company travel portal. Economy class is standard. Premium economy is allowed
for flights longer than six hours, and business class only for flights longer than ten hours with
director approval.

## Hotels
Hotel rates are capped at 180 EUR per night in most cities and 250 EUR per night in London, Paris, New York
and Tokyo. Book through the travel portal so the company can locate travellers in an emergency.

## Per diem
Travellers receive a daily meal allowance of 45 EUR for domestic trips and 60 EUR for international trips.
The per diem replaces individual meal receipts; alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Travel insurance
All business trips are covered by the company's group travel insurance, including medical emergencies and
cancellations. Download the insurance certificate from the travel portal before departure.

## Receipts for travel
Keep receipts for transport, hotels and visas and upload them with the trip's expense claim. Boarding passes
must be attached for flights booked outside the travel portal.
