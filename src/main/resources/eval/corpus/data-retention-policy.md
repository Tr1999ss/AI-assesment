# Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods
Customer contracts are kept for ten years after they end. Support tickets are kept for three years. Server
logs are kept for ninety days unless they are part of a security investigation.

## Customer data deletion
When a customer asks for their personal data to be erased under Article 17 of the GDPR, the request must be
completed within thirty days. Data in backups is erased when those backups expire.

## Backups
Database backups are taken daily and kept for thirty-five days. Monthly snapshots are kept for one year and
stored encrypted in a second region.

## Legal hold
Records under legal hold are excluded from every deletion process, including scheduled retention and
erasure requests, until the legal team lifts the hold in writing.
