# Employee Handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Up to five unused days may be carried
into the first quarter of the next year with manager approval; any remaining days lapse on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees who are unwell must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note
is required for absences longer than five consecutive working days. Sick leave is paid in full for up to
twenty working days per year.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave. Partners and adoptive parents receive eight
weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year.

## Remote work
Employees may work from home up to three days per week by agreement with their manager. Fully remote
arrangements require approval from the department head and HR. Home office equipment can be claimed once,
up to 400 EUR, through the expense system.

## Code of conduct
Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with HR or anonymously
through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary
offence.
